================================================================================
                        SUPPLYSYNC — SYSTEM FLOW EXPLANATION
                     Enterprise Supply Chain Management System
================================================================================


OVERVIEW
--------
SupplySync manages the complete procurement lifecycle from vendor registration
all the way through to payment. There are six roles in the system: Admin,
Vendor, Procurement Officer, Warehouse Manager, Finance Officer, and Compliance
Officer. Each role has its own dashboard and can only access what is relevant
to their job.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — VENDOR REGISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A vendor visits the public registration page and fills in their company details
such as company name, tax number, license number, contact information, and a
document URL. Once submitted, their account is created with a status of
"Pending". The system automatically sends a notification to all Procurement
Officers informing them that a new vendor is waiting for approval.

The vendor cannot do anything meaningful until they are approved. If they log
in, they will see a banner saying their account is pending.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — VENDOR APPROVAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Procurement Officer opens their dashboard and sees the pending vendor in
the Vendors tab (with a badge showing the count of pending approvals). They can
click on the vendor name to view full details including documents, tax number,
and license. They then either approve or reject the vendor.

If approved — the vendor receives a notification and their status changes to
"Approved". They can now participate in procurement.

If rejected — the vendor receives a notification with the rejection reason. The
vendor can log in, see the reason, update their details, and reapply. Once
reapplied their status goes back to "Pending" and Procurement Officers are
notified again.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — CONTRACT CREATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before any purchase orders can be raised, there must be an active contract with
the vendor. The Procurement Officer creates a contract by selecting an approved
vendor, setting start and end dates, and specifying payment terms, delivery
terms, and item pricing details.

The contract is first created in "Draft" status. The vendor receives a
notification that a contract has been created for them. The Procurement Officer
must then explicitly activate the contract, changing its status to "Active".
Only active contracts allow Purchase Orders to be created against them. Closed
contracts do not allow new POs.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — PURCHASE ORDER CREATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With an active contract in place, the Procurement Officer creates a Purchase
Order. They select the vendor, select the active contract, set an expected
delivery date, and add line items with quantities and unit prices. The total
is calculated automatically.

Once created, the PO status is set to "Sent" and the vendor immediately
receives a notification. The Procurement Officer can cancel a PO as long as
it has not yet been delivered.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — VENDOR DELIVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The vendor logs in and sees the Purchase Order on their dashboard. When they
have shipped the goods, they click "Mark Delivered" on the PO. This changes
the PO status to "Delivered" and signals the Warehouse Manager that goods are
on the way.

Until the vendor marks the PO as delivered, the Warehouse Manager cannot create
a Goods Receipt for it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — GOODS RECEIPT (WAREHOUSE MANAGER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Warehouse Manager sees the delivered PO in their "Incoming Deliveries" tab.
They click "Receive Goods" and are presented with a form showing all items from
the PO.

They must make one of two decisions:

  ACCEPT — All items are received in good condition. The system automatically
  adds the received quantities to inventory. The PO stays in "Delivered" status.
  The vendor receives a notification saying their delivery was received and they
  can now submit an invoice.

  REJECT — The goods do not meet requirements (wrong items, damaged shipment,
  etc.). The Warehouse Manager must provide a reason. The system creates a
  Goods Receipt with status "Rejected" and reverts the PO status back to "Sent".
  The vendor can see that their delivery was rejected, read the reason, and mark
  the PO as Delivered again once they re-ship. The cycle repeats until the goods
  are accepted.

Note: There is no partial acceptance. It is either fully accepted or fully
rejected to keep the process clean and unambiguous.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 — INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When goods are accepted, the inventory is automatically updated. If an item
already exists in the system by name, its quantity is increased. If it is a new
item, a new inventory record is created automatically.

The Warehouse Manager can also manually add inventory items, update stock, and
issue items to departments or employees. When stock for any item drops to 10
units or below after an issue, a low stock alert notification is sent to the
Warehouse Manager.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 — INVOICE SUBMISSION (VENDOR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once the Goods Receipt is accepted, the vendor can submit an invoice. They go
to their Invoices tab and click "Submit Invoice". They select the Purchase Order
(only delivered POs appear), then select the corresponding Goods Receipt (only
accepted GRs appear — rejected ones are hidden), and the total amount is
automatically calculated from the PO items. The vendor cannot change the amount
manually, preventing overcharging.

The invoice is submitted with status "Submitted". All Finance Officers receive
a notification immediately.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9 — THREE-WAY MATCHING AND INVOICE REVIEW (FINANCE OFFICER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Finance Officer receives a notification and opens the invoice. Before they
can approve, the system performs a three-way match check:

  1. The invoice must be linked to a valid PO that belongs to that vendor.
  2. The Goods Receipt linked to the invoice must have status "Accepted".
  3. There must not already be an approved invoice for the same PO and GR.
  4. The invoice must currently be in "Submitted" status.

If all checks pass, the Finance Officer can approve or reject the invoice.

If approved — the invoice status changes to "Approved" and the vendor receives
a notification. The invoice is now ready for payment.

If rejected — the Finance Officer provides a rejection reason. The vendor is
notified with the reason. The vendor cannot resubmit the same invoice but the
process can be restarted if needed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10 — PAYMENT PROCESSING (FINANCE OFFICER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For approved invoices, the Finance Officer sees a "Pay" button. They click it
and enter a payment reference number. The amount is fixed and cannot be changed
— it must match the invoice total exactly. Once confirmed, a Payment record is
created and linked to the invoice. The vendor receives a notification with the
payment reference number and amount.

Each invoice can only be paid once. Once paid, the invoice shows as "Paid" with
the reference and date.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 11 — COMPLIANCE AUDITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Compliance Officer can perform an audit check on any entity in the system
at any time — vendors, contracts, purchase orders, invoices, or inventory items.
They select the entity type, enter the entity ID, choose Pass or Fail, and
write their findings in the remarks field.

If the result is "Fail", the system immediately notifies all Admins and all
Procurement Officers so they can take corrective action.

All compliance checks are recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited or
deleted, providing a complete and tamper-evident compliance trail.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 12 — NOTIFICATIONS (ALL ROLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every significant event in the system generates a notification for the relevant
role. Notifications are shown in real time — the unread count in the top bar
updates automatically every 15 seconds without requiring a page refresh. Users
can click on a notification to mark it as read, or mark all as read at once.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 13 — REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Various roles have access to reporting:

  Vendor Performance  — Procurement Officers and Admins can see each vendor's
  total POs, delivered POs, invoice counts, and total spend.

  Procurement Spending — Finance Officers can see a breakdown of all approved
  invoices with payment references and a running total.

  Delivery Delays — Procurement Officers can see all POs that have passed their
  expected delivery date and are still not delivered, along with days delayed.

  Inventory Summary — Warehouse Managers can see current stock levels and how
  much of each item has been issued.

  Compliance Summary — Compliance Officers can see a count of Pass, Fail, and
  Pending checks, plus the 10 most recent failures.
